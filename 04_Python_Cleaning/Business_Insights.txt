E-Commerce Business Analytics – Key Business Insights

1. Customer Retention

Out of 96,096 unique customers, 93,099 were one-time customers and only 2,997 were repeat customers. The repeat customer rate is approximately 3.12%. This indicates an opportunity to improve customer retention through loyalty programs and targeted offers.

2. Customer Satisfaction

The average customer review score is 4.09 out of 5, indicating a generally positive customer experience.

3. Delivery Performance

The average delivery time is 12.09 days. The minimum delivery time was 0 days, while the maximum was 209 days. Improving delivery efficiency can help enhance customer satisfaction.

4. Revenue Performance

The total revenue was R$16,008,872.12 BRL (Brazilian Real). Monthly revenue analysis showed that November 2017 recorded the highest revenue among the analyzed months.

5. Payment Preferences

Credit cards were the most commonly used payment method, followed by boleto, voucher, and debit card. This indicates a strong preference for credit card payments.

Conclusion

The analysis highlights opportunities to improve customer retention, delivery performance, and revenue growth while maintaining customer satisfaction. These insights can support better business decisions.
